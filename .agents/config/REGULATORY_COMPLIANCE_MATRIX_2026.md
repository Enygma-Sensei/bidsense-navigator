## CRITICAL COMPLIANCE RULES FOR BIDSENSE

### Competition Act 1998 - Resale Price Maintenance (YOUR ISSUE)

**ILLEGAL CONDUCT:**
- ❌ Enforcing minimum resale price on resellers/PSLs
- ❌ Threatening to withdraw supply if reseller undercuts
- ❌ Penalizing resellers for selling below specified price
- ❌ Monitoring reseller prices and punishing discounts

**LEGAL CONDUCT:**
- ✅ Recommending a resale price (RRP)
- ✅ Setting maximum resale prices (if genuine, not acting as minimum)
- ✅ Resellers must be FREE to set their own prices

**ENFORCEMENT:**
- CMA (Competition and Markets Authority) enforces
- Fines up to 10% of worldwide turnover
- Individual director disqualification possible

**BIDSENSE ACTION:**
- Any reseller/PSL agreement with minimum price = ILLEGAL
- Change all minimum pricing to "recommended pricing"
- Add clause: "Resellers are free to set their own price below recommended amount"
- pricing_compliance_auditor agent must catch this in every contract
